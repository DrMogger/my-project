2.2.1 Lifestyles

LISTENING

Task 1. In pairs, discuss the questions and come up with 2-3 ideas.

  1. How can rich countries address regional inequality?
  2. As the cost of living rises, how can such “left behind” areas be made richer? 

Task 2. Watch the video on how to tackle regional inequality and do the tasks:

(a) Match the words to make up word collocations.

A     B  C                          
1. the scale   investment  
2. up-and-coming a new cluster economy   
3. enjoy to a brain drain  
4. a London-centric of schools  
5. make approach  
6. underachievement         of a problem   
7. avoid  a difference  
8. witness a firsthand of transformation

10. rewire an ecosystem  
11. build a fortune  
12. create the benefits of prosperity  
13. create catch-all approach  
14. lead  musicians

(b) Explain the meaning of the idioms or write synonyms to the words in bold.

1. feel the pinch 8. an economic powerhouse
2. be left in lurch 9. rejuvenate an area
3. change perceptions of an area 10. a flourishing hub
4. devolution of power 11. a pool of graduates
5. slash an unemployment rate 12. a good reason to stay put
6. a slam dunk  13. price some residents out
7. turn a corner 14. have the heft

(c) Describe:

  • Middlesbrough in 5 adjectives;
  • the role of Mayor using 5 negative adjectives;
  • Pitsburgh in 5 action verbs;
  • a cluster economy using 3 positive and 3 negative words (either nouns or adjectives);
  • the event in Germany that happened in 1989 and its significance;
  • possibilities of luring new residents to poor areas.
